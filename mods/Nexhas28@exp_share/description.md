
# Exp Share

Exp Share lets you configure how battle experience is distributed from the OPTIONS menu in Pokémon Gen 1 and Gen 2.

## Features

- **OFF:** restores the vanilla experience behavior.
- **GEN 1:** uses the classic Exp. All-style distribution.
- **GEN 5+:** fighters keep their full experience share while eligible party members receive additional experience.
- **BALANCED:** shares experience with lower-level party members.
- **AVERAGE:** uses the party's average level to determine who receives shared experience.
- **CUSTOM:** configure the global percentage or set individual percentages for party slots, from 10% to 100%.
- **SINGLE EXP SHARE:** limits sharing to a selected party slot or the entire bench.
- **LEVEL UP JINGLE:** choose the normal level-up fanfare or the shorter item pickup sound.

The settings are stored per save. The mod supports Pokémon Red, Blue, Yellow, and Gold.

Exp Share can also work alongside certain overhaul mods that modify the battle experience system.
