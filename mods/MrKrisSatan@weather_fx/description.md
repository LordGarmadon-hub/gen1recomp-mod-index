
# Weather FX

Weather FX adds dynamic weather effects to Pokémon Gen 1 and Gen 2 games supported by Gen1Recomp.

## Features

- Dynamic weather in the overworld and battles.
- Rain, storms, snow, clouds, lightning, and tornadoes.
- Seasonal effects and day/night atmosphere.
- Weather-related audio and atmospheric effects.
- Support for both 2D environments and compatible 3D voxel renderers.
- Automatic weather and weather-front systems.
- Weather-related battle effects, including typed residual effects and buffs or nerfs.

## Compatibility

Requires Gen1Recomp with a compatible game version. The manifest declares support for Generation I and Generation II.

Weather FX can integrate with supported voxel, battle-effects, and companion mods. Those integrations are optional and depend on the compatibility of the individual mods.

**Important:** Version 8.2.14 removes the former RAVE weather mode, its settings, soundtrack, and related rendering effects. Poke Rave is distributed separately.
