
# Stronger Trainers

Gen 1 gym leaders are too easy. This mod makes trainer battles more challenging while keeping teams and strategies appropriate to each battle.

## Features

- Six-Pokémon teams for gym leaders, the Elite Four, the Champion, Giovanni, and major rival battles.
- Hand-picked movesets with better type coverage.
- Smarter battle AI that chooses effective moves and heals more sensibly.
- Bosses can switch Pokémon to respond to unfavorable type matchups.
- Higher levels and fuller parties for ordinary trainers.
- Stronger static encounters, including the legendary birds and Mewtwo.
- Separate trainer rosters for Red, Blue, and Yellow.
- Configurable options under MODS > Stronger Trainers, including trainer levels, boss teams, switching, party size, and experience gain.

## Compatibility

Designed for Pokémon Red, Blue, and Yellow.

The author states that other trainer mods can work alongside it. Trainer Rematch uses vanilla teams for rematches, and experience mods can stack with its configurable experience setting.

The mod changes battle difficulty substantially. Consider adjusting its options if you find the early game too difficult.
