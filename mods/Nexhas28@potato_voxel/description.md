
# PotatoVoxel

PotatoVoxel transforms the Pokémon overworld into a 3D voxel diorama, with terrain depth, shadows, and adjustable quality presets.

Features include HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW, POTATO, and CUSTOM graphics modes, plus a persistent terrain cache designed to reduce repeated map-building work.

The mod supports Generation I and Generation II according to its manifest. Performance depends on your device, game version, resolution, and other enabled mods.

**Compatibility warning:** Do not enable PotatoVoxel alongside other voxel-world mods, including DramaticShape, Dramaless Shape, or other voxel forks. They modify overlapping systems and may cause visual glitches, crashes, or performance problems.
