# Untamed Advanced

A FireRed and LeafGreen port of pokeemerald-expansion's Pokémon follower and overworld wild encounter systems, with encounter chaining inspired by Emerald Rogue.

Your lead Pokémon can follow you and react when spoken to. Wild Pokémon appear in grass and water, with different behaviors such as wandering, chasing, or fleeing. Bumping into them starts a battle, and defeating the same species consecutively builds a chain that improves its encounter rate and shiny odds. Pokémon already placed in the world also use matching overworld sprites and idle animations.

Most features can be toggled in the mod's options.
