
# QoL Toggles

QoL Toggles adds a QOL TOGGLES submenu to the OPTIONS screen, with configurable quality-of-life improvements for Generation I and II games.

Features include encounter and Repel options, capture and healing changes, field-move and HM rules, battle EXP and money multipliers, menu navigation improvements, and other gameplay conveniences.

Each toggle can be configured independently and saved with your preferences.
