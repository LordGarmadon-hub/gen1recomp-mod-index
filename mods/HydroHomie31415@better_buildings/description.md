
# Better Buildings

Better Buildings redraws Kanto's buildings in a FireRed-inspired architectural style.

Features include:

- Distinctive designs for Pokémon Centers, Poké Marts and all eight gyms.
- Redesigned houses and civic buildings tailored to each town.
- New designs for route gates, Silph Co., Pokémon Tower and Pewter Museum.
- Preserved building footprints, collision, doorways and warps.
- Compatibility with supported voxel-diorama mods.

The mod also works in the flat overworld. For the voxel diorama, it integrates with Battle Art Voxel, DramaticShape, Dramaless Shape or PotatoVoxel.

**Compatibility note:** These voxel-diorama mods conflict with one another. Use only one of them at a time. Better Buildings is designed to integrate with whichever supported option you have installed.
