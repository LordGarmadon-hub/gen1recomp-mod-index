# Untamed Tohjo

Replaces random encounters with wild Pokémon that appear directly in the overworld. Pokémon can roam, be encountered by walking up to them, and vary by time of day; the system also supports surfing, fishing, and legendary roamers.

Build encounter chains by battling or catching the same species. Shiny Pokémon and alternate forms appear on the map, and the Incense menu lets you adjust spawn density or focus on roamers. An optional follower system lets you choose a party member to accompany you, react to its condition and surroundings, and find items while walking.

Supports Gen 1 and Gen 2 games, and is designed to work with expanded Pokédex mods.
