
# Wilds of Kanto Revival

Wilds of Kanto Revival makes Kanto feel alive with visible Pokémon roaming the overworld, reacting to the player, and following your party.

## Features

- Visible wild Pokémon in grass, caves, and water.
- Optional classic step-based encounters.
- Shiny Pokémon with configurable encounter rates.
- Party followers, with support for up to six additional Pokémon.
- Swimming and floating follower sprites.
- Peaceful Pokémon in towns and buildings.
- Multiple sprite styles, including Poké Followers / GSC and HGSS / PokeMMO.
- Support for Pokémon species through Generation 9 when paired with compatible dex and sprite mods.

## Compatibility

Supports Pokémon Red, Blue, and Yellow. Pokémon Gold support is experimental.

Do not enable this mod alongside the original Wilds of Kanto (`overworld_wild_spawns`) or the separate Shiny Pokémon mod (`SHINY_POKEMON`), because this version replaces those overlapping features.

Compatible voxel renderers include supported Battle Art, Potato, Dramaless Shape, and Terrarium variants.
