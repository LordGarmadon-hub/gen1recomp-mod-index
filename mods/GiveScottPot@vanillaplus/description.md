
# Vanilla+

Vanilla+ is a spoiler-light expansion for Pokémon Red, Blue, and Yellow on Gen1Recomp++.

It adds quality-of-life improvements, optional encounter changes, new world interactions, inventory tools, and additional content while aiming to preserve the original Generation I experience.

## Features

- Running Shoes and additional travel conveniences.
- Optional expanded wild encounters and encounter settings.
- Battle EXP progress and caught-species indicators.
- Reusable TMs, an organized TM/HM Bag, and the Adventurer's Toolkit.
- Expanded inventory storage.
- Additional NPC interactions and trade evolution services.
- A Legal Move Tutor and Move Deleter.
- Additional exploration and postgame content.

## Compatibility and safety

The mod targets Gen1Recomp++ versions >=0.2.56 and <2.0.0, according to its manifest.

The author reports testing specific combinations with Wilds of Kanto, PotatoVoxel, and DRAMALESS SHAPE. Compatibility is not guaranteed for every version or combination of mods.

Back up important saves before major updates or changing your mod setup.
